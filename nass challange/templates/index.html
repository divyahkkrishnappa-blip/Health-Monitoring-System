THE MISSION

Five hazards. One human.

Spaceflight brings a distinct set of interacting challenges. Explore NASA’s human spaceflight hazard framework.

01 / RADIATION

Space radiation

Energetic particles can affect crew health and spacecraft systems.

 02 / ISOLATION

Isolation & confinement

Small crews and confined living conditions can shape well-being.

 03 / DISTANCE

Distance from Earth

Greater distance challenges communication and access to care.

 04 / GRAVITY

Gravity fields

Microgravity and transitions affect the body in multiple ways.

 05 / ENVIRONMENT

Closed environments

Controlled habitats require careful attention to environmental health.
